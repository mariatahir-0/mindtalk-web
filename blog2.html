Breathing is something we do every moment, yet most of us rarely notice it. Conscious breathing can instantly relax your body and clear your mind. Here are a few simple breathing exercises that you can try anywhere, anytime.

1. Deep Belly Breathing

Sit or lie comfortably. Place a hand on your stomach and inhale deeply through your nose. Feel your belly rise, then exhale slowly through your mouth. Repeat for one minute to calm your nervous system.

2. Box Breathing

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds, and hold again for 4 seconds. This pattern helps restore balance and focus — a favorite of athletes and professionals.

3. 4-7-8 Technique

Inhale through your nose for 4 counts, hold for 7 counts, and exhale through your mouth for 8 counts. It slows your heartbeat and releases stress quickly.

4. Alternate Nostril Breathing

Use your right thumb to close your right nostril, inhale through the left. Then switch — close your left nostril with your ring finger and exhale through the right. Repeat this for balance and clarity.

5. Mindful Observation

Simply observe your breath — notice how it feels as it enters and leaves your body. This awareness brings you into the present moment and releases tension.

Even one minute of focused breathing can shift your energy and mood. Try adding these exercises to your day whenever you need a quick reset.
