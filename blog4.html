Getting good sleep is essential for your physical and mental well-being. Here are simple, science-backed tips to help you sleep better every night.

1. Create a Consistent Sleep Schedule

Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, even on weekends. This helps regulate your body’s internal clock and improves sleep quality.

2. Limit Screen Time Before Bed

The blue light from screens can trick your brain into thinking it’s still daytime. Try avoiding phones or laptops at least 30 minutes before bed.

3. Keep Your Room Cool and Dark

A dark, quiet, and slightly cool environment signals your body it’s time to rest. Use curtains, eye masks, or white noise if needed.

4. Avoid Caffeine and Heavy Meals

Drinking coffee or eating heavy food late in the evening can make it harder to fall asleep. Choose light snacks or herbal tea instead.

5. Try Relaxation Techniques

Practice deep breathing, meditation, or light stretching before bed to calm your mind and release tension.
